## Maker's first comment (post as soon as the launch goes live)
```text
Hi Product Hunt! I'm Yaron, and I made Imgkeep.

The problem: Chrome and Edge now often save images as WebP or AVIF, and plenty of apps still won't open them. The most popular fix, the "Save image as Type" extension (1M+ users), was sold in late 2025 and removed from the Chrome Web Store as malware in March 2026 after it started injecting affiliate links.

So I built a replacement with one rule: it should be impossible for it to do that.

What it does
• Right-click any image → PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF (animated images stay animated)
• Saves straight into a folder you pick, with your own file names, and never overwrites anything
• WebP and AVIF become files every app opens

Why you can trust it
• Only 4 permissions, and no access to the pages you visit
• The only thing it downloads is the image you clicked; conversion happens on your computer
• No ads, no tracking, no account, no review pop-ups
• Open source (MIT), so anyone can check

It's free on Chrome and Edge. I'd love your feedback: what's missing, what's confusing, what breaks. There's a public ideas board where you can suggest and vote on features: https://imgkeep.app/ideas.html
```

## Launch day
- A Product Hunt day starts at **12:01 am Pacific**, which is **10:01 am in Israel** (while Israel is on summer time; 11:01 am after it ends). Schedule the launch for that time so it gets the full 24 hours.
- **Tuesday to Thursday** gets the most visitors and the most competition; the weekend is quieter, so it's easier to rank but there's less traffic. For a small, free tool, a weekday is still the usual choice.
- Reply to every comment during the day.
- **Don't ask anyone for upvotes**, and don't post the link in groups asking for votes. Product Hunt penalises vote-asking. Telling people "I launched on Product Hunt today, feedback welcome" is fine.
