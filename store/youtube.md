## Description
```text
Chrome saves many images as WebP or AVIF, and lots of apps won't open them. Imgkeep adds one right-click menu: save any image as PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF (animation included), straight into the folder you choose.

➜ Add to Chrome (free): https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fkclfgbmjaafglfifenonfcahfdmajbl?utm_source=youtube
➜ Website: https://imgkeep.app
➜ Source code (MIT): https://github.com/ybider-tech/imgkeep

What it does
• WebP and AVIF → JPG or PNG that every app opens
• Animated WebP, AVIF and APNG → GIF that keeps moving
• Saves to your folder, with your file names; never overwrites
• JPG and WebP quality you control

Built to be trusted
• Only 4 permissions and no access to the pages you visit
• Images are converted on your computer; nothing is uploaded
• No ads, no tracking, no account, no review nags
• Open source, so anyone can check what it does

Guides
• Why Chrome saves images as WebP: https://imgkeep.app/chrome-saves-images-as-webp/
• Save WebP as JPG or PNG: https://imgkeep.app/save-webp-as-jpg-png/
• Save AVIF as JPG or PNG: https://imgkeep.app/save-avif-as-jpg-png/

Questions or ideas: https://imgkeep.app/support.html

#ChromeExtension #WebP #GIF
```
