# Imgkeep privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Imgkeep is a browser extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge that saves the image you right-click as PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF.

## What Imgkeep collects

Nothing. Imgkeep collects no personal data.

## What Imgkeep reads

Imgkeep reads only the image you right-click and choose to save. It converts that image on your computer and uploads nothing. It has no access to the pages you visit.

## Where your settings live

Your settings (save mode, subfolder, file-name template, quality and background colour) are stored in your browser's sync storage. If sync is on in Chrome or Edge, the browser copies them across your devices. If you choose a folder, the browser's handle to that folder is stored locally in the extension's IndexedDB and never leaves your computer.

## Access to sites

Some sites block other sites from reading their images. Only then, and only after you click a button, Imgkeep asks your browser for access to that one site, to fetch the images you pick there. You can remove that access at any time in Imgkeep's Options, under "Sites you've allowed".

## What Imgkeep never does

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be announced before it ships.
