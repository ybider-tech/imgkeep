    Short answer

    Drop a JPG above, choose ICO and download one .ico file that holds the image at 16, 32 and 48 pixels, plus 256 pixels when your image is at least that big. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded. A JPG has no transparency, so the icon keeps the JPG's background; a non-square image is centred on a transparent square.

    Updated 25 November 2026

    Will a JPG make a good icon?

    It works, with one catch: JPG has no transparency, so a logo on a white JPG background becomes a white square icon. If you have the logo as a PNG or SVG with a transparent background, use PNG to ICO or SVG to ICO instead.

    What size should the JPG be?

    Square and at least 256 × 256 pixels gives the sharpest result and adds the 256 px size Windows uses for large icons. Crop photos to a square around the subject first: at 16 px only bold shapes stay recognisable.

    How do I use it as a favicon?

    Upload it to the root of your website as favicon.ico, and browsers find it on their own. For a desktop shortcut on Windows, open the shortcut's Properties, choose Change Icon and pick the file.

    Questions

    Which sizes are inside the .ico?

    16, 32 and 48 px, plus 256 px when the JPG is at least 256 px. Each is stored as a PNG inside the icon.

    Can I choose the sizes?

    Not yet. Every .ico holds the standard sizes above.

    Why is my icon blurry at 16 px?

    16 pixels is tiny, so detail disappears. Simple shapes and strong contrast work best.
