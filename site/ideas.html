Suggest a feature See and vote on ideas

How it works

  1. Look first. Your idea may already be there. If it is, click the ⬆ arrow on it to add your vote.
  2. Suggest it. If it's new, fill in the short form: what you'd like to do and why it matters to you.
  3. Follow along. Replies and progress appear right on the idea, and shipped ideas are listed in each release's notes.

No GitHub account?

The ideas board lives on GitHub, and posting or voting needs a free GitHub account. If you'd rather not create one, email your idea to helloimgkeep@gmail.com and we'll add it to the board for you.

Privacy

The board is hosted by GitHub, so GitHub's privacy policy applies there. This site still has no tracking or analytics.
