How to save images from X (Twitter)

Short answer

On X, each photo sits under a link to the post, so right-clicking gives you link options rather than Save image as. The image address also sets the size, like name=small or name=large. Open the image's address, change it to name=orig, and save it. With Imgkeep, use Find image here or Alt+S, and turn on Save the full-size version for the original.

How X works was checked on 24 November 2026, on x.com in Chrome. X changes often; if something here looks different, tell us at helloimgkeep@gmail.com.

Why can't I save an image from X with right-click?

X puts a link over each photo in a post, so a right-click lands on the link and the menu offers Open link in new tab and similar, not Save image as.

The images come from pbs.twimg.com, and the end of the address picks the size and format, for example ?format=jpg&name=small, medium or large. name=orig asks for the size it was uploaded at.

Animated "GIFs" on X are really short videos, so image tools, Imgkeep included, can't save them as images.

How do I save an image from X without an extension?

  1. Right-click the photo and choose Inspect. Next to the highlighted link, find the <img> whose address starts with pbs.twimg.com/media/.
  2. Open that address in a new tab and change name=small (or medium, large) to name=orig.
  3. Save the image from that tab. Change format=webp to format=jpg if you need a JPG.

Or take a screenshot, at the size shown on screen.

How do I save it with Imgkeep?

Imgkeep is a browser extension that saves images as PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or PDF from the right-click menu. We make it. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by X.

  1. Right-click the photo and choose Imgkeep: Find image here…. Imgkeep finds the image under the link and opens it in More options, where you pick JPG, PNG or another format, then Save or Copy as PNG.
  2. Or point at the photo and press Alt+S (Option+S on a Mac, Alt+Shift+S in Firefox) to save it in the format you used last.
  3. Turn on Save the full-size version in Options and Imgkeep asks X's image service for name=orig, the uploaded size, and saves the image you clicked if it can't get it.

Find image here only finds an image that's already shown on the page. It's off until you turn it on, and Imgkeep only looks at the page when you ask.

Can I use the images I save?

Saving an image to look at later, for a mood board or your own reference, is different from reusing it. Images belong to the people who made them, and X's terms apply to what's posted there. Before you post, print or sell an image, ask its creator and credit them. Only save what you can already see; Imgkeep doesn't get past sign-ins, private accounts or paywalls, and isn't meant to.
