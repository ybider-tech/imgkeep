How it works

  1. 01

    Right-click an image

    Find an image on the web and right-click it.

  2. 02

    Pick your format

    Choose PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (keeps animation), PDF or original. Or copy it as PNG to paste anywhere.

  3. 03

    Save to your folder

    Imgkeep converts it on your computer and saves it where you want, every time.

Features

Everything it does.

  • Remembers your folder

    Pick a folder once. Every image lands there, in subfolders you name, and nothing is ever overwritten.

  • Handles WebP and AVIF

    Turn the WebP and AVIF files sites serve into PNG or JPG that every app opens. Animations stay animated as GIF.

  • Copy as PNG

    Put the image on your clipboard as a real PNG, transparency kept, and paste it into chat, docs or design tools like Canva, Figma and Google Slides. How.

  • Your file names

    Name files with the original name, the site, the date or the size, like {name}-{w}x{h}.

  • Works where right-click doesn't

    On sites like Instagram and Pinterest, photos sit under another layer. Choose Find image here and Imgkeep finds the photo at its largest size. Off until you turn it on.

  • Resize and preview

    Set a maximum width and big images shrink to fit. More options shows the result and its real file size before you save, with quality and background you choose.

Why Chrome saves WebP

Chrome saves the exact file a site sends, and many sites send WebP because it's smaller. No Chrome setting changes that: convert the image as you save it, or afterwards in Paint or Preview.

Chrome saves JFIF

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Imgkeep free?
Yes. Everything Imgkeep does today is free, with no ads and no limits. A paid Imgkeep Pro with extra features is planned, and the current features stay free.
Does Imgkeep upload my images?
No. Each image is converted on your computer and saved straight to your folder. The only thing Imgkeep sends is one anonymous daily count of which features were used and which saves failed, and you can turn it off in Options. Privacy policy.
Which formats can it save?
PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (animation kept), PDF or the original format. It converts the WebP and AVIF images many sites now serve, and Copy as PNG puts an image on your clipboard.
Can it save images that sites cover, like on Instagram or Pinterest?
Yes. Right-click the photo and choose Imgkeep: Find image here. Imgkeep looks at that page once, finds the photo under your click at its largest size, and opens it ready to save. It's off until you turn it on, and it never changes a page.
Which browsers does it work in?
Google Chrome 116 and newer, and Microsoft Edge, in 9 languages.
Can it save every image on a page at once?
No. Imgkeep saves the one image you right-click. For whole pages you need a bulk downloader.
