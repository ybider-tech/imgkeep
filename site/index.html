Imgkeep

Right format. Right folder. Nothing else.

Save any image as PNG, JPG or WebP

Right-click any image on the web, pick a format, done. Imgkeep converts it on your computer and saves it where you want, every time. WebP and AVIF included, PDF when you need a document, and Copy as PNG when you just want to paste it.

Add to Chrome — free Also for Microsoft Edge → Works in Chrome 116+ and Microsoft Edge, in 9 languages. Four permissions, no access to your pages.

What it does

Remembers your folder

Pick a folder once. Every image lands there, in subfolders you name, and nothing is ever overwritten.

Handles WebP and AVIF

Turn the WebP and AVIF files sites serve into PNG or JPG that every app opens. Animations stay animated as GIF.

Your file names

Name files with the original name, the site, the date or the size, like {name}-{w}x{h}.

Quality you control

Set JPG and WebP quality, and the background colour transparent images get as JPG.

Copy as PNG

Put the image on your clipboard as a real PNG, transparency kept, and paste it into chat, docs or design tools.

Resize and preview

Set a maximum width and big images shrink to fit. More options shows the result and its real file size before you save.

Built to be trusted

No access to your pages

Imgkeep can't read or change the sites you visit. It sees only the image you right-click.

Nothing leaves your computer

Conversion happens on your device. No uploads, no analytics, no account.

Open source

Every line is public under the MIT licence, with no build step. Read it before you install.

Not for sale

No ads, no data deals. One request for a rating, after you've used it a while, and never again. Every free feature stays free.
