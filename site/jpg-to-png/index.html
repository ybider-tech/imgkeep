    Short answer

    Drop your JPGs above, keep PNG selected and download the PNGs. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded. The PNG keeps every pixel of the JPG exactly, so editing it later loses nothing more; it can't bring back detail the JPG already lost, and the file is usually larger. A JPG has no transparency, so the PNG has none either.

    Updated 25 November 2026

    Why convert JPG to PNG?

    Some apps, upload forms and print services ask for PNG. PNG is also the better working copy if you'll edit the image several times: each time you save a JPG it compresses again, while a PNG saves exactly what you see.

    JPG and PNG compared

    FormatJPGPNG
    CompressionLossy: small photo filesLossless: every pixel kept
    TransparencyNo (filled with a colour)Yes, with soft edges
    AnimationNoNot in a plain PNG
    Opens inEvery app, browser and upload formEvery app and browser

    Will the PNG be better quality?

    No better than the JPG, and no worse. The PNG stores the JPG's pixels exactly, including any blur or blockiness from its compression. If you have the original photo, convert that instead.

    Why is the PNG so much bigger?

    PNG compresses without throwing anything away, which suits graphics and screenshots but not photos. A photo as PNG is often three to five times the size of the JPG. To make it smaller, set a Maximum width in Advanced options; to keep files small, stay with JPG.

    Can I make the background transparent?

    Not with a converter: a JPG has no transparent areas, so there's nothing to keep. Removing a background needs an image editor. For a logo that already has transparency, start from its PNG, SVG or WebP version.

    Questions

    Is converting JPG to PNG lossless?

    The conversion itself is: the PNG holds exactly the JPG's pixels. It can't restore what the JPG lost when it was first saved.

    Can I convert a PNG back to JPG?

    Yes, with PNG to JPG, for a smaller file to share.

    Can I convert several JPGs at once?

    Yes, up to 20 at a time, each up to 50 MB. Download them one by one or all together.
