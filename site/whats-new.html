What's new in Imgkeep

Every release, newest first, and what's coming next. Your browser updates Imgkeep by itself, usually within a day of a release. New right-click items say New for a month after an update.

Coming next

  • Imgkeep Pro: presets for the sizes and formats you use most, file-size targets like "under 200 KB", rules per site, and a log of where every image came from. Join the waitlist. Everything that's free today stays free.
  • Your ideas: the most-voted ideas get built first. Suggest or vote.

0.10.0 · 16 October 2026

  • Save as AVIF: in More options, pick AVIF for files that are often half the size of a JPG that looks the same, with transparency kept. Browsers can open AVIF but can't create it, so Imgkeep now includes libavif, the open-source reference encoder. It's the one compiled part of Imgkeep, included unchanged and checked by its fingerprint, and it only loads when you save as AVIF. Thanks to everyone who asked for it.

0.9.0 · 15 October 2026

  • Save as ICO: in More options, pick ICO to get one icon file with the image at 16, 32 and 48 px (and 256 px when it's big enough), ready for desktop shortcuts and favicons. Thanks for the suggestion.
  • Save as BMP: also in More options, a plain bitmap that every app opens.
  • The anonymous daily summary moved to PostHog. It went from Aptabase to PostHog, an open-source analytics service, in its EU cloud, because it shows us the numbers far more clearly. Nothing else changed: still one summary a day, the same counts, never images, addresses, site or file names, and still one click to turn it off in Options. Better than before: PostHog doesn't store your IP address, and no country or city is recorded either. Details in the privacy policy.

0.8.1 · 14 October 2026

  • The anonymous daily summary also counts what gets converted into what, like "WebP to JPG", so we know which conversions to make better first. It reads the format from the image itself; still never addresses, site or file names. The full list is in the privacy policy.

0.8.0 · 12 October 2026

  • Find image here: on Instagram, Pinterest and similar sites, photos sit under another layer, so the right-click can't reach them. Right-click and choose Imgkeep: Find image here, and the photo opens ready to save, at its largest size. It's off until you turn it on. Imgkeep only looks at a page when you ask it to find an image there, just that page, just then, and never changes a page.

0.7.1 · 12 October 2026

  • If you ever remove Imgkeep, your browser opens a short, optional form asking why. Two questions, no email, nothing sent by Imgkeep itself. It helps us fix what made people leave.

0.7.0 · 10 October 2026

  • Your last format, first: the format you saved in last is offered again at the top of the right-click menu, like "JPG (last used)", always in the same spot. Thanks to the Reddit user who suggested it.
  • Anonymous daily summary: once a day, Imgkeep sends a summary like "12 saves as JPG, 1 failed" to Aptabase, an open-source analytics service hosted in the EU, so we know which features to improve and which saves fail. Never images, addresses, file names or anything that identifies you. It's on by default: Options → Anonymous usage counts shows exactly what's sent and turns it off with one click. Your images still never leave your computer. The full list is in the privacy policy.

0.6.1 · 9 October 2026

  • New right-click items are labelled New for 30 days after an update, so you can spot them.
  • What's new and Rate Imgkeep links in Options.
  • After you've used Imgkeep for a while, it asks once whether you'd like to rate it. Answer either way and it never asks again. The counting happens on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

0.6.0 · 6 October 2026

  • Copy as PNG: put the image on your clipboard as a real PNG, transparency kept, and paste it anywhere.
  • Maximum width: set it in Options and bigger images are scaled down to fit, keeping their shape. Smaller ones are never enlarged.
  • More options…: preview the result with its real size in pixels and kilobytes, then pick format, width, quality, background and file name for just this image.
  • Save as PDF: a one-page PDF exactly the size of the image. Transparency is kept.
  • 9 languages: English, Arabic, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish.
  • A new icon, and clearer errors with Try again and Open image.

0.4.0 · September 2026

  • GIF (keeps animation): animated WebP, AVIF and APNG become GIFs that keep moving. Animated GIFs are saved exactly as they are.
  • Available for Microsoft Edge too.

0.3.0 · September 2026

  • First release: save any image as PNG, JPG or WebP, into the folder you choose, with your own file names.

Every change, in detail: the changelog on GitHub.
