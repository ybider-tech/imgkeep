What's new in Imgkeep

Every release, newest first, and what's coming next. Your browser updates Imgkeep by itself, usually within a day of a release. New right-click items say New for a month after an update.

Coming next

  • Anonymous usage counts, in 0.7.0: to learn which features people use and whether saves fail, Imgkeep 0.7.0 will send one anonymous summary a day, like "12 saves as JPG, 1 failed", to Aptabase, an open-source, privacy-first analytics service hosted in the EU. Never images, page addresses, file names or anything that identifies you. It will be on by default, and one click in Options turns it off. Your images still never leave your computer.
  • Imgkeep Pro: presets for the sizes and formats you use most, file-size targets like "under 200 KB", rules per site, and a log of where every image came from. Join the waitlist. Everything that's free today stays free.
  • Your ideas: the most-voted ideas get built first. Suggest or vote.

0.6.1 · October 2026

  • New right-click items are labelled New for 30 days after an update, so you can spot them.
  • What's new and Rate Imgkeep links in Options.
  • After you've used Imgkeep for a while, it asks once whether you'd like to rate it. Answer either way and it never asks again. The counting happens on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

0.6.0 · 6 October 2026

  • Copy as PNG: put the image on your clipboard as a real PNG, transparency kept, and paste it anywhere.
  • Maximum width: set it in Options and bigger images are scaled down to fit, keeping their shape. Smaller ones are never enlarged.
  • More options…: preview the result with its real size in pixels and kilobytes, then pick format, width, quality, background and file name for just this image.
  • Save as PDF: a one-page PDF exactly the size of the image. Transparency is kept.
  • 9 languages: English, Arabic, French, German, Hebrew, Hindi, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish.
  • A new icon, and clearer errors with Try again and Open image.

0.4.0 · September 2026

  • GIF (keeps animation): animated WebP, AVIF and APNG become GIFs that keep moving. Animated GIFs are saved exactly as they are.
  • Available for Microsoft Edge too.

0.3.0 · September 2026

  • First release: save any image as PNG, JPG or WebP, into the folder you choose, with your own file names.
