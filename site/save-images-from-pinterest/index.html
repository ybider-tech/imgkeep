How to save images from Pinterest

Short answer

Pinterest covers its images with another layer, so right-clicking a pin usually doesn't offer Save image as, and the image in the grid is a smaller copy. Open the pin and use Pinterest's own menu if it offers a download, or open the image's address and swap the size for originals. With Imgkeep, choose Find image here, or press Alt+S over the pin.

Updated 24 November 2026

How Pinterest works was checked on 24 November 2026, on pinterest.com in Chrome. Pinterest changes often; if something here looks different, tell us at helloimgkeep@gmail.com.

Why won't Pinterest let me right-click an image?

On Pinterest the right-click usually lands on a layer above the picture, not on the picture itself, so the browser shows the page menu without Save image as. The image is still there underneath.

The image you see is also a resized copy. Pinterest's image addresses include the size, like i.pinimg.com/236x/… in the grid or /736x/ and /1200x/ for larger views. The same image is usually there at its uploaded size under /originals/.

Signed out, clicking a pin opens Pinterest's sign-up box. Everything below assumes you're signed in and can see the pin.

How do I save a Pinterest image without an extension?

  1. Pinterest's own menu. Open the pin and look in its ⋯ menu. If it offers Download image, that's the simplest way.
  2. The image's address. Right-click the pin, choose Inspect, and find the <img> next to the highlighted layer. Open its address in a new tab, change the size part (236x, 736x…) to originals, and save the image from that tab. If originals doesn't load, keep the largest size that does.
  3. A screenshot. Win+Shift+S on Windows, Cmd+Shift+4 on a Mac. It always works, but only at the size shown on screen.

Pinterest images are often WebP or JPG. If an app won't open the file, see how to save WebP as JPG or PNG.

How do I save it with Imgkeep?

Imgkeep is a browser extension that saves images as PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or PDF from the right-click menu. We make it. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.

  1. Right-click the pin and choose Imgkeep: Find image here…. Imgkeep finds the image under the layer, at the largest size the page offers, and opens it in More options, where you pick PNG, JPG or another format, then Save or Copy as PNG.
  2. Or point at the pin and press Alt+S (Option+S on a Mac, Alt+Shift+S in Firefox) to save it straight away in the format you used last.
  3. For the uploaded size rather than the copy on screen, turn on Save the full-size version in Options. Imgkeep then asks Pinterest's image service for the originals file, and saves the image you clicked if it can't get it.

Find image here only finds an image that's already shown on the page. It's off until you turn it on, and Imgkeep only looks at the page when you ask.

Can I use the images I save?

Saving an image to look at later, for a mood board or your own reference, is different from reusing it. Images belong to the people who made them, and Pinterest's terms apply to what's posted there. Before you post, print or sell an image, ask its creator and credit them. Only save what you can already see; Imgkeep doesn't get past sign-ins, private accounts or paywalls, and isn't meant to.

Questions

How do I download a Pinterest image in full size?

Open the image's address and change the size part, like 736x, to originals. With Imgkeep, turn on Save the full-size version in Options and it does that for you, falling back to the image you clicked if the original isn't available.

Why is Save image as missing on Pinterest?

Your right-click lands on a layer Pinterest puts over the image, so the browser shows the page menu instead of the image menu. The image is still on the page, underneath.

Does the pin's creator know I saved their image?

Saving an image in your browser doesn't send anything to Pinterest or the creator. Using it is another matter: ask the creator before you reuse it.
