Every conversion, one page each

The Free Image Converter works in your browser: your images are never uploaded. Each page below opens it set to one conversion, with what that conversion keeps and loses.

From HEIC

  • HEIC to JPG: Turn iPhone photos into JPGs that open on Windows and in any upload form. They're converted on your device: nothing is uploaded.
  • HEIC to PNG: Turn iPhone photos into PNG files that open anywhere. They're converted on your device: nothing is uploaded.
  • HEIC to PDF: Turn iPhone photos of documents, receipts and IDs into PDFs, without uploading them.

From WebP

  • WebP to JPG: Convert WebP to JPG without uploading: your images stay on your device, and there's no account.
  • WebP to PNG: Convert WebP to PNG without uploading, transparency kept. Your images stay on your device.
  • WebP to GIF: Turn animated WebP into a GIF that keeps moving, for chats and apps that only play GIF.

From AVIF

  • AVIF to JPG: Convert AVIF to JPG without uploading, for apps and forms that won't open AVIF.
  • AVIF to PNG: Open AVIF images in any app: convert them to PNG, transparency kept, without uploading.
  • AVIF to GIF: Turn animated AVIF into a GIF that plays everywhere, without uploading it.

From JFIF

  • JFIF to JPG: Turn .jfif files into standard .jpg files that every app and upload form accepts, without uploading.
  • JFIF to PNG: Turn .jfif images into PNG files for editors and apps that want PNG, without uploading.

From PNG

  • PNG to JPG: Convert PNG to JPG without uploading, for smaller files. Your images stay on your device.
  • PNG to ICO: Make a favicon or Windows icon without uploading: one .ico with all the common sizes.
  • PNG to WebP: Make PNG images smaller for your website, transparency kept, without uploading them.
  • PNG to PDF: Turn screenshots, diagrams and graphics into a PDF, transparency kept, without uploading.

From JPG

  • JPG to PNG: Turn a JPG into a PNG for an app or form that wants PNG, without uploading it.
  • JPG to PDF: Make a PDF from a photo or scan for a form or an email, without uploading it.
  • JPG to ICO: Make a favicon or Windows icon from a photo or logo JPG, without uploading.

From SVG

  • SVG to PNG: Turn an SVG logo or icon into a PNG for apps and sites that can't open SVG, without uploading.
  • SVG to ICO: Make a sharp favicon or Windows icon from your SVG logo, transparency kept, without uploading.
