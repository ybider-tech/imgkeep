Imgkeep Pro is coming

For people who save a lot of images to the same specification: product photos, listings, blog posts, design references. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once when it's ready.

Join the waitlist by email

What Pro adds

  • Presets. Save the formats and sizes you use most, like "Product photo: JPG, 1600 px wide, under 500 KB", and pin them to the right-click menu.
  • File-size targets. Ask for "under 200 KB" or "under 1 MB" and get the best quality that fits, or a clear message when it can't.
  • Rules per site. Send each site's images to the right folder, in the right format, with the right name, without choosing every time.
  • A log. Where every image came from, so you can find the source later.

What stays free

Everything Imgkeep does today, and everything in version 0.6.0: saving as PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF and PDF, Copy as PNG, maximum width, More options with a live preview, your folder and file names. Free features never move behind a paywall.

Privacy

The waitlist is just an email to us. We use your address only to tell you when Pro is ready, and you can ask us to delete it any time. This site still has no tracking or analytics.
