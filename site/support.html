Want a new feature? Suggest it or vote on the ideas board. Questions or bugs: email helloimgkeep@gmail.com. Please include the page address and what you tried to save.

Where are Imgkeep's settings?

Open Chrome's Extensions menu (the puzzle icon), click ⋮ next to Imgkeep, then Options.

A small window asked to allow a site

Some sites block other sites from reading their images. Allow and save gives Imgkeep access to that one site only. You can remove it any time in Options, under "Sites you've allowed". Or choose Save original format instead.

It asks me to reconnect my folder

Chrome forgets folder access after a restart unless you chose Allow on every visit. Click reconnect once and pick that option.

An image won't save

Some images only exist inside the page (a blob: link). Imgkeep has no access to your pages, so it can't read those.

My animation stopped moving

PNG, JPG and WebP save one still frame. To keep an animation moving, choose GIF (keeps animation). Animated GIFs are saved exactly as they are; animated WebP, AVIF and APNG are converted frame by frame. GIFs are made up to 800px wide, and animations longer than 600 frames are too big to convert.

Like Imgkeep?

Imgkeep is free and has no ads. If it helps you, a rating on the Chrome Web Store helps other people find it. It's the only thing we'll ever ask for, and only here.

Privacy

Imgkeep collects no data and uploads nothing. Read the privacy policy.
