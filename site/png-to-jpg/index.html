    Updated 17 October 2026

    How much smaller will it be?

    It depends on the image. Photos usually shrink a lot; flat graphics with few colours can even grow. The converter shows the real size before and after for every file, so you can see which one you're getting.

    When should I keep PNG?

    For text, logos, screenshots and anything with sharp edges or a transparent background. JPG blurs sharp edges slightly and can't be see-through.

    What happens to a transparent background?

    JPG can't store transparency, so transparent areas get the background colour you pick above. White by default, but any colour works, for example to match the page you'll put the image on.

    Can I resize at the same time?

    Yes: set a maximum width before converting. Wider images are scaled down, keeping their shape; smaller ones are never enlarged.

    Questions

    Does PNG to JPG lose quality?

    A little: JPG compresses, PNG doesn't. At the default quality of 92 it's usually invisible in photos, but sharp text and lines can look slightly soft.

    Can I convert JPG back to PNG?

    Yes, pick PNG in the menu above. It won't bring back detail the JPG already lost, but nothing more is lost.

    How many files can I convert at once?

    Up to 20 at a time, each up to 50 MB.
