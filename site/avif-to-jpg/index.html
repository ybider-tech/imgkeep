    Updated 17 October 2026

    How do I convert AVIF without uploading?

    Drop the files on this page. Your browser opens the AVIF and the page writes a JPG, on your device: no server sees your images, and it works offline once the page has loaded.

    Why can't my apps open AVIF?

    AVIF is newer than most apps and upload forms. Browsers show it, but many editors, older systems and websites still refuse it. See how to save AVIF as JPG or PNG.

    JPG or PNG for AVIF?

    JPG for photos and the smallest files that open everywhere. PNG when the image has a transparent background you want to keep, or when you'll edit it again: pick PNG in the menu above.

    Which browsers can convert AVIF?

    Any browser that can show AVIF: Chrome and Edge, Firefox, and Safari on recent versions of macOS and iOS. If yours can't open the file, the converter says so.

    Can I skip the AVIF file next time?

    Yes. With the Imgkeep extension, right-click the image on the web page and choose Imgkeep: Save image as › JPG, and you never have the AVIF at all.

    Questions

    Why is my JPG bigger than the AVIF?

    AVIF compresses much better than JPG, so the same picture usually takes more space as a JPG. Lower the quality or set a maximum width for a smaller file.

    Does it handle animated AVIF?

    JPG keeps one frame. To keep the motion, pick GIF in the menu above (in Chrome and Edge).

    Can I convert AVIF to PNG?

    Yes: pick PNG in the menu above. PNG keeps transparency and every pixel.
