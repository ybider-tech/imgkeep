How to save images from Instagram

Short answer

Instagram covers every photo with another layer, so the right-click never reaches the image and there's no Save image as. For your own photos, download your information from Instagram. For a photo you can see in the browser, find its address in developer tools, take a screenshot, or use Imgkeep's Find image here or Alt+S.

Updated 24 November 2026

How Instagram works was checked on 24 November 2026, on instagram.com in Chrome. Instagram changes often; if something here looks different, tell us at helloimgkeep@gmail.com.

Why can't I right-click an Instagram photo?

On instagram.com each photo sits under another layer, so a right-click lands on the layer and the browser shows the page menu, without Save image as. Instagram has no download button for other people's photos either.

Signed out, Instagram also covers profiles and posts with a sign-in box. Everything below is for photos you can see while signed in. Private accounts are private: nothing here is a way around that.

How do I save an Instagram photo without an extension?

  1. Your own photos. Instagram lets you download a copy of what you've posted: in Accounts Center, under Your information and permissions, choose Download your information.
  2. The image's address. Open the post, right-click the photo and choose Inspect. Find the <img> near the highlighted layer; its srcset lists several sizes. Open the largest in a new tab and save it there.
  3. A screenshot. Win+Shift+S on Windows, Cmd+Shift+4 on a Mac, at the size shown on screen.

How do I save it with Imgkeep?

Imgkeep is a browser extension that saves images as PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or PDF from the right-click menu. We make it. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram.

  1. Open the post, right-click the photo and choose Imgkeep: Find image here…. Imgkeep finds the image under the layer, at the largest size the page offers, and opens it in More options: pick JPG, PNG or another format, then Save or Copy as PNG.
  2. Or point at the photo and press Alt+S (Option+S on a Mac, Alt+Shift+S in Firefox) to save it in the format you used last.
  3. In a post with several photos, go to the one you want first: Imgkeep saves the photo on screen.

Find image here only finds an image that's already shown on the page, and Instagram videos and Reels aren't images, so it can't save them. It's off until you turn it on, and Imgkeep only looks at the page when you ask.

Can I use the images I save?

Saving an image to look at later, for a mood board or your own reference, is different from reusing it. Images belong to the people who made them, and Instagram's terms apply to what's posted there. Before you post, print or sell an image, ask its creator and credit them. Only save what you can already see; Imgkeep doesn't get past sign-ins, private accounts or paywalls, and isn't meant to.

Questions

Can you save Instagram photos on a computer?

Not with Save image as: a layer covers each photo. Use the image's address from developer tools, a screenshot, or an extension like Imgkeep that finds the photo under the layer.

How do I download all my own Instagram photos?

Use Download your information in Instagram's Accounts Center. It gives you a copy of what you've posted, without any extension.

Does Instagram tell people when I save their photo?

Saving a photo in your browser with a screenshot or an extension doesn't send anything to Instagram. Reusing it is another matter: ask the person who posted it.
