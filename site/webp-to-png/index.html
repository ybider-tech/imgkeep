    Why is the PNG bigger than the WebP?

    PNG is lossless: it stores every pixel exactly, while WebP usually compresses with small, invisible losses. The converter shows the real size before and after. To make the PNG smaller, set a maximum width, or choose JPG if there's no transparency to keep.

    When is JPG better?

    For photos without transparency: JPG files are much smaller and every app opens them. Use WebP to JPG.

    Is the transparency really kept?

    Yes. PNG keeps transparent and partly transparent areas exactly. The preview shows them over a checkerboard, so you can see what's see-through before you download.

    How do I keep an animation?

    PNG holds one picture, so an animated WebP becomes its first frame. Pick GIF in the menu above to keep the motion (in Chrome and Edge; GIFs are at most 800 px wide and 600 frames), or see how to save an animated WebP as a GIF.

    Can I paste it into Canva, Figma or Google Slides?

    Yes: use Copy as PNG on the result and paste it. Google Slides and Docs don't list WebP among the formats they take, and PNG works everywhere. More in getting an image into Canva, Figma or Google Slides.

    Questions

    Does WebP to PNG lose quality?

    No. PNG is lossless, so the PNG shows exactly what the WebP showed.

    Why won't Google Slides take my WebP?

    Google's help lists PNG, JPG and GIF for images, not WebP. Convert to PNG, or copy the result as PNG and paste it.

    Can I make the PNG smaller?

    Yes: set a maximum width before converting, or convert to JPG if the image has no transparency.
